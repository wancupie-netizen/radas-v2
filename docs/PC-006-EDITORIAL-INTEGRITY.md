# PC-006 Editorial Integrity

Affiliate Playbook is presented inside the published Research Detail page.

## Explicit fields

- Product Pain: AI draft, editable by admin.
- Suitable For: AI draft, editable by admin.
- Content Angles: title, hook and rationale.
- Execution Playbook: ordered actions and notes.
- Reference Videos: optional admin-supplied URLs only.

## Integrity metadata

- `reviewed`: admin has verified the published research.
- `limited_information`: evidence is incomplete.
- `update_required`: published information needs a new review.

Only an admin may publish or modify published research. AI never sets the item to published.
